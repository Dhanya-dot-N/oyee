# oyeee: a voice companion that talks to you in your own words

**Built for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI**
Dhanya, second-year CS student, Bengaluru

## The problem

Many students know exactly what they need to do and still can't start. The gap between knowing and starting is where hours disappear. Most productivity apps respond with generic alarms and a voice that isn't yours. For anyone who freezes, nagging adds guilt, and guilt makes starting harder.

## The idea

oyeee is a desktop companion that speaks to you as **another you**. You save the excuses you usually make ("I don't want to do DSA") and the replies you would actually believe, in your own words. When you say what you're avoiding, oyeee answers with your wording, shrinks the task into one 5-10 minute step, and checks in until you're done. It never guilts, shames or roasts, and it is an accountability tool, not a therapist.

## How it works

- **Know Me:** save excuses with 2-3 replies each, plus a "why I'm doing this" note. Everything can be spoken instead of typed.
- **Talk to Me:** speak or type what you're avoiding. The reply uses your saved words, names one small chunk, and suggests a method that fits (5-minute start, Pomodoro, ugliest first draft). It uses deadlines and progress you mention, so "2 hours left, half done" gets a concrete next step.
- **Nudge Me:** say "assignment due 10pm, write at 5pm" and it creates two reminders. A calendar shows today and the days ahead, and times can be edited.
- **Check-ins:** after you press **Start**, oyeee checks in at 10, 20 and 30 minutes, then every 30 minutes up to 3 hours, with a clear "last check-in". Each one is spoken aloud with **Done**, **Still going** and **I'm stuck** buttons. Three unanswered check-ins and it goes quiet instead of nagging. "Started" is always reported by you and never claimed by the app.

## Where Gnani AI fits

Voice is the whole experience, so Gnani is the core of the product, not an add-on:
- **Prisma (speech-to-text)** turns what the user says into text in English, Hindi and Kannada, for excuses, replies, tasks and reminders.
- **Timbre v2.5 (text-to-speech)** speaks every reply, reminder and check-in back in the same language.
- Google Gemini writes the reply text and turns spoken reminders into structured times.

## Why it matters

Students in India think and speak in more than one language, and most tools are built for typed English. A companion that listens and answers in Hindi or Kannada, in the user's own phrasing, feels like a friend and not a system. The same design suits anyone who struggles to begin tasks.
